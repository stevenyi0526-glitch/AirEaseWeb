## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how C&K Capital Limited ("we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the AirEase mobile application ("App"). By using AirEase, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Location Data
With your permission, AirEase collects your device's geographic location to identify nearby airports and provide relevant flight search results. Location data is used only while the App is in active use and is not stored on our servers.

### 2.2 Usage Data
We may collect non-personally identifiable information about how you interact with the App, including search queries, feature usage patterns, and device type. This data is used solely to improve App performance and user experience.

### 2.3 Information You Provide
If you contact us for support, we may collect your name and email address to respond to your inquiry.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected to:

- Provide and improve flight search results based on your location
- Enhance App features and fix technical issues

- Comply with applicable legal obligations

## 4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share data with:

- **Service Providers**: Third-party vendors who assist in operating the App (e.g., flight data APIs, analytics providers), subject to confidentiality agreements

## 5. Location Data

AirEase requests access to your device location only when the App is in use ("When In Use" permission). We do not track your location in the background. You may revoke location access at any time through your device's Settings app. Revoking location access may limit certain App features.

## 6. Data Retention

We do not persistently store your location data. Non-identifiable usage analytics may be retained for up to 12 months to improve App performance, after which they are deleted.
